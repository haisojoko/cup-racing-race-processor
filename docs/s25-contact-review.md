## Measurements

**Time** is after the green; **replay** is the clock inside the `.acreplay` file, for scrubbing straight to the moment. **Along** and **across** are the gaps between car centres measured along the road and across it, at the instant of contact. **Alongside** applies the league definition - the attacking car's front axle ahead of the defending car's rear axle, which on this car's 3.6 m wheelbase means an along-road gap under 3.6 m - and says how long that overlap had existed before impact. **Brake** is how much later the following car braked than the car ahead; a negative figure means the following car braked first. **Speeds** are ahead/behind.

## How this was measured

The method and its traps are written up in [wheel-to-wheel-rules.md](wheel-to-wheel-rules.md). In short:

- The server records each contact with both cars, an impact speed and a map position, but **no lap and no timestamp**. Each one was dated by finding the replay frame where the hitting car sat on that spot with the other car touching it.
- Positions, throttle and brake all come from the replay, per car. The throttle and brake channels were validated first: brake over 50% gives -10 to -16 m/s2 and full throttle +3.8 to +4.3 m/s2, which is right for this car.
- The two cars are compared **at the same point on the road**, not at the same instant, because a follower reaches a given point up to 0.2 s later and a bend would otherwise read as a driver moving across the track.
- A car's width is taken as 2.0 m and the wheelbase as 3.6 m, from the Supreme 25.
- Movement across the road is always stated relative to the other car, with the direction taken from the sign of the measurement rather than written by hand.

### What to distrust

- There is **no steering channel** in the replay. "Moved towards" means the car's measured path across the road, not a steering input.
- Verdicts that turn on 1.8 m against 2.0 m sit inside the tolerance of the assumed car dimensions. That is why those are warnings rather than penalties.
- The server's impact speed disagrees with the measured closing speed in slow, queued contacts. Speeds in the tables are measured from the replay; the impact figure is the server's.
- The last 0.15 s before any contact is the impact itself, not driving.
- **Off-track abuse is not covered at all.** `Laps[].Cuts` is empty in every league race file, so the server is not recording cuts and there is nothing to review.
- "Defenders may not move too late" and "closing the door last second" are reserved to steward discretion by the rules. This document says what happened and when; the call is yours.
